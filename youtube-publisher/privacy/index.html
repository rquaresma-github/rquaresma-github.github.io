YouTube Publisher

Privacy policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

In short

YouTube Publisher is a private tool that publishes our own videos to our own YouTube channels. Only we use it. It collects nothing about viewers or anyone else, has no analytics or tracking, and we don’t sell or share any information.

Who we are

Maple Drift Studios is the name under which Rodrigo Paulo Quaresma, an independent developer in Canada, makes games and videos; it isn’t a company. Rodrigo runs YouTube Publisher, is responsible for this policy, and is the person in charge of privacy (the privacy officer), reachable at the address under Contact.

What it accesses

When we sign in with Google and allow it, the tool uses the YouTube Data API on our own channels to upload our videos and thumbnails, set their titles, descriptions, tags, language and schedule, create and update our playlists, and update our channels’ descriptions and keywords. It reads the signed-in channel’s name and handle only to confirm it is publishing to the right channel. It doesn’t read comments, subscribers, viewers or any other account.

What it stores

Only the authorization Google issues for our own channels, kept privately: as encrypted secrets in our private code repository, and on our own computer. The videos wait in that private repository until they are published, then they are deleted from it. Nothing about any other person is stored.

Google API Services

YouTube Publisher uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Its use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The tool’s access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or Google security settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
