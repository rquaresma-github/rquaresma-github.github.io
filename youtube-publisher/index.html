YouTube Publisher

YouTube Publisher is a private tool that publishes our own videos to our own YouTube channels:

  • Mestre dos Astros (@MestredosAstros), The Star Wisdom (@TheStarWisdom) and Maestro de los Astros (@MaestroDeLosAstros)
  • Smart Money Ops (@SmartMoneyOps)
  • Nerd 3D Print (@Nerd_3DPrint)

It uploads each video with its title, description, tags, thumbnail and schedule, adds it to the channel's playlists, and keeps the channels' descriptions and keywords up to date. Only Maple Drift uses it, and only on its own channels; it isn't offered to anyone else.
