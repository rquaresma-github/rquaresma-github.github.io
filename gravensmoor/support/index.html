Controls

Pick a control mode in the game menu’s Options (the button at the right end of the top bar):

  • Casual: drag anywhere and Wren follows your finger, shooting while you touch. Tap a dropped weapon to change it.
  • Gamer: steer with a stick anywhere on the screen and hold the FIRE button with your other thumb. The FIRE button can sit on the left for left-handed players.
  • Game controllers (Xbox, PlayStation, MFi) work too: the stick or D-pad moves, A fires, X drinks a potion, Y throws a bomb, and B leaves a menu.

Difficulty (Gentle, Normal or Hard) can be changed in Options at any time, and takes effect at once.

Saving and continuing

The game saves every time Wren reaches a village, and after anything you do there. Choose Continue on the title screen to carry on from the last village you reached. Progress on a road isn’t saved halfway, so a road always starts from its beginning.

Your journey is saved on this device only. It isn’t synced to your other devices through iCloud, but it is included in your device’s own backups, so restoring a backup to a new phone brings it with you. Deleting the game deletes your journey.

When Wren falls

When his hearts run out, you have ten seconds to continue. Tap to go back to the village the road set out from, with full hearts, to try again or take another road. You lose the coins found on that road since it began. Shops and kitchens sell potions, armor and meals that help on hard roads.

Stuck?

Talk to the villagers: they tell you where blocked paths lead and what opens them, and everything they say is written in the Diary in the game menu. The Map shows the roads you’ve found.

Purchases

The whole game can be played and finished for free. Its one purchase, Pip’s Patron, is optional: it makes you the bard’s patron, with the keepers’ gifts at once and no ad, free portals, Pip’s songbook, and the dream after an ending. If you bought it and it’s missing, open Options, choose Pip’s Patron, and tap Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing is supported.

Ads

Ads only play when you choose to watch one, for a keeper’s gift in a shop, kitchen or forge. If none is offered, no ad is ready yet: check your connection and try again soon. Patrons get the gifts with no ad at all. In regions that ask for ad consent, you can change your choice in Options → Pip’s Patron → Ad privacy. See the privacy policy for details.

Game Center

Achievements and the guardian rush leaderboards use Game Center. If they don’t appear, check that you’re signed in to Game Center in your iPhone’s Settings, then reopen the game.

Language

Gravensmoor follows your iPhone’s language when it has it: English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese. You can pick another in Options; the game restarts in it and your journey carries on from the last village.
