Controls

Pick a control mode in the game menu’s Options (the button at the right end of the top bar):

  • Casual: drag anywhere and Wren follows your finger, shooting while you touch. Tap a dropped weapon to change it.
  • Gamer: steer with a stick anywhere on the screen and hold the FIRE button with your other thumb. The FIRE button can sit on the left for left-handed players.
  • Game controllers (Xbox, PlayStation) work too: the stick or D-pad moves, A fires, X drinks a potion, Y throws a bomb, and B leaves a menu.
  • A keyboard works too: the arrow keys or WASD move, Space fires, Q drinks a potion, E throws a bomb, Tab opens the game menu and Esc leaves a menu.

On a phone or tablet the game starts in Casual. You can switch at any time.

Difficulty (Gentle, Normal or Hard) can be changed in Options at any time, and takes effect at once.

Saving and continuing

The game saves every time Wren reaches a village, and after anything you do there. Choose Continue on the title screen to carry on from the last village you reached. Progress on a road isn’t saved halfway, so a road always starts from its beginning.

Your journey, medals and settings stay on this device only: they don’t follow you to other devices and aren’t in your Google Account’s backups. Deleting the game, or clearing its storage in your device’s settings, deletes them.

When Wren falls

When his hearts run out, you have ten seconds to continue. Tap to go back to the village the road set out from, with full hearts, to try again or take another road. You lose the coins found on that road since it began. If he falls fighting a guardian, you keep them, and continuing tries the fight again. Shops sell bottles and amulets, kitchens fill bottles with potions and cook meals for the road, and forges sell weapons, armor and shields: they all help on hard roads.

Stuck?

Talk to the villagers: they tell you where blocked paths lead and what opens them, and everything they say is written in the Diary in the game menu. The Map shows the roads you’ve found.

Purchases

The game is free to start: the tale is free up to the Mist Wraith. Its one purchase, Pip’s Patron, continues the song: the rest of the tale to the Last Tower, the keepers’ gifts at once and no ad, Pip’s songbook and the dream. If you bought it and it’s missing, check that you’re signed in to Google Play with the same Google Account you bought it with, then reopen the game.

Ads

Ads only play when you choose to watch one, for a keeper’s gift in a shop, kitchen or forge. If none is offered, no ad is ready yet: check your connection and try again soon. Patrons get the gifts with no ad at all. In regions that ask for ad consent, you can change your choice in Options → Ad privacy. See the privacy policy for details.

Google Play Games

Achievements and the guardian rush leaderboards use Google Play Games. If they don’t appear, check that you’re signed in to Google Play Games on your device, then reopen the game.

Language

Gravensmoor follows your device’s language when it has it: English, Brazilian Portuguese, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Ukrainian and Turkish. You can pick another in Options; the game restarts in it and your journey carries on from the last village.
