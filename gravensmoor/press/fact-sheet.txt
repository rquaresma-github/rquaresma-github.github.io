Developer   Maple Drift Studios, an independent one-person studio in Canada

Controls    Touch, keyboard and game controllers
Game Center 30 achievements, rush leaderboards
Price       Free to start. Pip's Patron, one purchase ($4.99), continues the tale

THE GAME
A vertical-scrolling shooter told as a folk tale: Wren, a young ranger, crosses an
open valley of roads and villages to win back the Great Lantern from Hundred-Eye.
Pip, a bard, sings it in rhyme over a chiptune score.

- Fifteen guardians
- Six weapons
- A dozen villages
- Roads walked both ways
- Hidden roads and villages

FREE TO START
Free up to the Mist Wraith, a third of the valley. Ads only play when a player asks
for a keeper's gift, and the game never asks to track.

LANGUAGES
Twelve: English, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, German, French, Italian, Japanese,
Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), Turkish and Ukrainian.

RELEASE
Out since Oct 30 2026 in Canada, Australia and New Zealand.
From Nov 16 2026 also in the US, the UK, Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Macao,
Turkey, Ukraine, Brazil, the Spanish-speaking countries of Latin America and
Switzerland. The EU and mainland China are not part of this launch.
