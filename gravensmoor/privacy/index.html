Gravensmoor

Privacy policy

Last updated 3 October 2026

In short

Gravensmoor has no accounts and no servers of its own. We don’t collect your name, email or anything else about you. Your journey stays on your device. Ads come from Google AdMob, and only play when you choose to watch one.

Your journey

Your journey, road medals, guardian rush times and settings are saved on your device only. They’re included in your device’s own backups (such as iCloud Backup), which Apple handles; we can’t see them. Deleting the game deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free, and in shops, kitchens and forges you can watch a short ad for a keeper’s gift (Pip’s Patrons get the gifts with no ad). Ads only play when you tap a button that says so. To show ads, Google collects your IP address and approximate location (never your precise location), identifiers for your device, how you interact with ads, and crash and performance data. Gravensmoor never asks for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites, so Google doesn’t get your device’s advertising identifier from it. In the EU, the UK and other regions that require it, you’re asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from Options → Pip’s Patron → Ad privacy. Google’s use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The game’s one purchase, Pip’s Patron, is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; the game only learns whether you own it.

Game Center

If you’re signed in to Game Center, your achievements and guardian rush times are sent to Apple’s Game Center and shown to other players according to your Game Center settings.

Statistics

The game counts things like time played and roads attempted and cleared on your device, to help us balance it. These counts stay on your device and are never sent to us. If you’ve chosen to share data with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple gives us anonymous totals such as installs and crashes.

Children

Gravensmoor is a general-audience game and isn’t directed at children under 13. The game itself collects nothing from anyone.

Your choices

You can play the whole game without watching an ad, change ad consent from Options where it applies, reset your advertising settings in your iPhone’s Settings, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new date.
