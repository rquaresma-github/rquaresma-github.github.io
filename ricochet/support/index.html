Support

Aim, bounce, break every brick.

Need help or found a bug? Email mapledrift.support@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and, for a level, its number. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Store (home page footer or the pause menu) and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with. Purchases are one-time and work on all your devices.

What's free and what's in the full game?

Levels 1–20 and Endless up to level 50 are free. The full game unlocks levels 21–100, the daily challenge and Endless past level 50. Speed control and the long aim line are separate one-time items, or together as Pro tools.

Is my progress saved? Does it move to a new phone?

Progress is saved on your device after every turn. It isn't synced between devices, and deleting the app deletes it. Purchases are never lost: restore them from the Store.

How do the daily challenge and leaderboards work?

A new board appears every day at your local midnight, the same board for every player. Sign in to Game Center (Settings → Game Center) to post your scores and see the leaderboards from the home page.

How are stars earned?

Clear a level in fewer shots for more stars. The level preview shows how many shots each star needs. Using a rescue limits the clear to one star.

There's no sound.

Check the sound button in the game, and make sure the iPhone's silent switch is off. Game sounds follow it.

A level feels too hard.

Try power-ups (you get free ones as you play, and can trade coins for more), flat shots that bounce along the bricks, and Undo. If one level still feels unfair, email us its number. We tune levels from feedback.
