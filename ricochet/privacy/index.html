Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026

Ricochet Bricks is made by Rodrigo Quaresma ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play. The short version: we don't collect any personal data.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't have servers that receive information from the app.

Game progress

Your levels, stars, scores, coins, power-ups and settings are stored only on your device. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this progress.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple which items your Apple Account owns, so it can unlock them and restore them on a new device.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the app sends your scores to Game Center leaderboards. Game Center is run by Apple: your Game Center nickname and scores may be visible to other players, as set in your Game Center settings. We don't receive your Apple Account details. You can sign out of Game Center in the Settings app at any time; the game works the same without it.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Apple's privacy policy

Purchases and Game Center are covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
