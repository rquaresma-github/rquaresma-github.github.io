Privacy Policy

English · Português · Español

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information the Loose Threads puzzle game ("the game", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Rodrigo Paulo Quaresma, trading as Maple Drift Studios, Ontario, Canada, who is responsible for it. Contact: support@mapledriftstudios.com.

In short: you don't create an account with us, we don't run any servers, and we don't collect your personal information ourselves. Your progress is saved on your device, and synced through your own Apple or Google account if you're signed in. The only other company that receives data from the game is Google, through AdMob, to show the optional ads you choose to watch.

1. Information stored on your device

To save your game, the game stores on your device: the patterns and daily patterns you've finished, spools kept, your daily streak, thimbles and hints, items from the in-game store, and your settings (language, sound, vibration, reminders and so on). We never receive them.

The home-screen widget reads your streak from the same device. Daily reminders are notifications scheduled on your device; nothing is sent to us.

2. Sync and backups

iPhone and iPad

If you're signed in to iCloud, the same game data is synced through Apple's iCloud key-value storage so it follows you to your other devices. It lives in your iCloud account, under Apple's Privacy Policy; we can't see it.

Android

If you're signed in to Google Play Games, the same game data is kept as a saved game in your Play Games account (stored by Google in your Google Drive, hidden from your files) so it follows you to your other Android devices. If Google backup is turned on, Android may also include it in your device backup. Both are handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; we can't see them.

3. Game Center and Google Play Games

If you're signed in to Game Center (Apple) or Google Play Games, the game sends your achievements and leaderboard scores to that service. Your player name and scores may be visible to other players there, according to your Game Center or Play Games settings. This is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy policies.

4. Purchases

Thimble packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment; we never receive your payment details. We only receive confirmation that the purchase is valid, so the thimbles can be added.

5. Ads (Google AdMob)

Ads only ever play when you choose to watch one, for a free hint or a free rethread. They're provided by Google AdMob. When the game loads or shows an ad, Google's software in the game may collect:

  • device identifiers (see below);
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (for example, your country);
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • how you interact with ads, and diagnostic information about ad performance.

iPhone and iPad

The identifier for vendors, and the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking when the game asks.

Android

Your device's advertising ID and app set ID. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in your device settings.

Google uses this to show, measure and improve ads and to prevent fraud, as an independent controller. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Your choices about ads

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the first time ads load, Google's consent form asks whether ads may be personalised and data used for the purposes listed there. Personalised ads are only shown if you agree. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices.
  • Not watching ads at all: ads are always optional. Everything in the game can be played without them.

iPhone and iPad

The game asks Apple's "Allow tracking?" question. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", your advertising identifier isn't shared. You can change this in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android

To stop apps from using your advertising ID, open your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices, Settings → Google → Ads) and delete or reset it.

7. What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't use analytics or crash-reporting services. (If we add one, we'll update this policy first.)
  • We don't access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or precise location.

8. Legal bases

Where privacy laws require a legal basis (such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR or Brazil's LGPD): keeping and syncing your game data is necessary to provide the game you asked for; purchases rely on the contract with you; personalised advertising, and storing or accessing information on your device for advertising, rely on your consent where that is required, which you can withdraw at any time as described above; otherwise ads rely on our legitimate interest in funding a free game.

9. Retention

Game data stays on your device, and in your iCloud or Play Games account, until you delete it. You can erase your progress in Settings → Your progress → Reset progress, or remove all local data by deleting the game.

iPhone and iPad

iCloud data can be managed in the iPhone Settings app, under your name → iCloud.

Android

Play Games data can be deleted in the Google Play Games settings, and device backups in your Google account.

Data collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies.

10. International transfers

Apple and Google may process information in countries outside your own, including the United States. They state that they use appropriate safeguards for such transfers, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example Brazil, the United States including California, Canada, the United Kingdom, Mexico, Argentina, Colombia, Chile or Australia), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your personal information, to object to or limit its use, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD). Because we hold no personal information about you on our side, most requests are fastest through the settings above, Apple or Google. You can also write to support@mapledriftstudios.com and we'll help.

12. Children

Loose Threads isn't directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us.

13. Changes

We'll update this page when the game changes how it handles information, and change the date above.
