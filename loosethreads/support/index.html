How to play

Tap a thread to pull it out the way its needle points. If another thread or a button is in the way, it snags and you lose a spool. Lose all three and you can rethread or start over. Clear every thread to finish the pattern.

  • Knots: tap once to loosen, then again to pull.
  • Buttons: block threads, and pop off once no thread touches them.
  • Double needles: tap near the end you want the thread to leave by.
  • Tied pairs: leave together, so both need a clear way out.
  • Starched threads: stiff until a thread beside them has been pulled.

Stuck? The bulb shows a free thread, and the grid button draws guide lines to help you line up needles.

Progress and iCloud

If you’re signed in to iCloud, your progress, thimbles and store items sync to your other devices automatically and come back if you reinstall. To start over, use Settings → Your progress → Reset progress (your thimbles are kept).

Purchases

Thimble packs are bought through the App Store. If a purchase was charged but no thimbles arrived, open the Store again while online, as delayed purchases are credited when they come through. For refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem, since Apple handles all payments.

Ads and privacy

Ads only play when you choose to watch one, for a free hint or a rethread. To change your ad choices, go to Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices in the app (shown where the law requires it), or iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the privacy policy for details.

Daily reminders and the widget

Turn reminders on or off and pick the time in Settings → Daily reminder. To add the widget, touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Loose Threads.
