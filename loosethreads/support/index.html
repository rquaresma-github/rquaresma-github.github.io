How to play

Tap a thread to pull it out the way its needle points. If another thread or a button is in the way, it snags and you lose a spool. Lose all three and you can rethread or start over. Clear every thread to finish the pattern.

  • Knots: tap once to loosen, then again to pull.
  • Buttons: block threads, and pop off once no thread touches them.
  • Double needles: tap near the end you want the thread to leave by.
  • Tied pairs: leave together, so both need a clear way out.
  • Starched threads: stiff until a thread beside them has been pulled.

Stuck? The bulb shows a free thread, and the grid button draws guide lines to help you line up needles.

Will my progress move to a new device?

iPhone and iPad

If you're signed in to iCloud, your progress, thimbles and store items sync to your other devices automatically and come back if you reinstall.

Android

If you're signed in to Google Play Games, your progress, thimbles and store items are kept in your Play Games account and come back on your other Android devices and if you reinstall.

To start over, use Settings → Your progress → Reset progress (your thimbles are kept).

I bought thimbles and they didn't arrive

Open the Store again while online: delayed purchases are credited when they come through.

iPhone and iPad

For a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem, since Apple handles all App Store payments.

Android

For a refund, use your Google Play order history, since Google handles all Google Play payments.

Ads and privacy

Ads only play when you choose to watch one, for a free hint or a rethread. To change your ad choices, go to Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices in the game (shown where the law requires it).

iPhone and iPad

Tracking is set in the iPhone Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android

Your advertising ID can be reset or deleted in your device's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

See the privacy policy for details.

Daily reminders and the widget

Turn reminders on or off and pick the time in Settings → Daily reminder.

iPhone and iPad

To add the widget, touch and hold your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Loose Threads.

Android

To add the widget, touch and hold an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, and find Loose Threads.
